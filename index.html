Procurement risk analytics dashboard

Master of Business Analytics · led the dashboard component · Power BI

A Power BI dashboard on a simulated enterprise procurement scenario: 1,000 purchase orders migrating between legacy and target systems, where mapping failures cause invoice delays and financial exposure.

The question behind it

The goal was to tell someone what to do on Monday morning, not just to describe the data. Every view leads to an owner and an action: which business unit is breaching SLA, which projects carry the most exposure, and which root causes are worth automating first.

Four views

  • Executive: mapping success, SLA breaches by business unit, top at-risk projects by exposure
  • Operational: invoice readiness against goods-receipt ageing, rework by root cause, a filterable action list
  • Financial: exposure by business unit, risk band and rework cause
  • Automation: a what-if slider estimating SLA breaches and minutes saved as automation coverage grows
What I'd do differentlyThe dashboard explains the current state but doesn't predict. The next step is to model which purchase orders are likely to fail mapping before they do, so the tool prevents problems as well as counting them.

A local business discovery app

Business Analyst · led end to end · Analyzen Bangladesh Ltd.

A client wanted a directory app where people find local businesses and owners list themselves. The brief arrived as a broad idea, so the first job was deciding what version one should be.

What I did

  • Ran MVP planning and requirements gathering directly with the client
  • Mapped the user flow on both sides of the marketplace: consumers searching and saving, owners onboarding and managing listings
  • Designed a seven-step owner onboarding flow, breaking one long form into stages so owners don't abandon it partway
  • Wrote the requirements specification, including permission states, empty states and confirmation for destructive actions
  • Led UI/UX design, worked with engineers on business logic, and ran UAT before release

A microfinance ERP platform

Business Analyst · Analyzen Bangladesh Ltd.

An NGO client delivering microcredit to rural communities needed the 2.0 release of the system that runs its whole lending operation. Real money, regulatory obligations, and field staff working offline in remote areas.

What I did

  • Mapped the operational lifecycle end to end (see the stepper)
  • Gathered 2.0 requirements and specified loan product configuration: instalment structures, eligibility rules, approval authority by role
  • Specified how transactional data flows into a reporting layer, so operations, audit and MIS teams each get the view they need
  • Documented approval hierarchies, user access and audit trail requirements with the client's compliance team
  • Built Power BI reporting on SQL-consolidated data
What I learnedMost of the value came from asking questions nobody had asked before. Several rules that "everyone knew" turned out to differ between branches once written down. The specification work surfaced process problems that predated the software.

A consumer travel booking platform

Business Analyst – Executive · FirstTrip Limited · 100,000+ downloads

A B2C air ticketing website and app on Google Play and the App Store. Fares come from several supplier systems that behave differently, payment can fail halfway, and a ticket that doesn't issue automatically becomes a support problem within minutes.

What I did

  • Documented requirements for the web portal and mobile app, and built the UI/UX frameworks that guided development
  • Mapped the booking journey from search to post-booking changes
  • Specified failure paths as well as the happy path: delayed issuance, fare changes between selection and payment, bookings that need manual help
  • Designed self-service cancellation and date-change flows
  • Analysed booking data with SQL and Excel, and coordinated integrations with airline, GDS and payment partners

A decision I'd call out: refunds

Users want money back now. The business wants to keep it. We offered both routes openly, with honest timelines on each, and let users choose.

Research

How certain is a neural network?

Four peer-reviewed conference papers from an undergraduate research group at BRAC University (2021–2023), led by Md Farhadul Islam and supervised by Meem Arafat Manab and Annajiat Alim Rasel. Each paper compares how different architectures express predictive uncertainty, using Monte Carlo Dropout.

My contribution

Model modification and architecture experiments, designing and revising result visualisations, solving experimental problems as they came up, and drafting and revising manuscript sections.

Research interests

Uncertainty quantification in deep learning, approximate Bayesian methods, calibration and reliability across architectures, and uncertainty-aware modelling for spatial and environmental prediction such as digital soil mapping.
